# Title 35 novelty: five absences, two unary and three binary, on one head

Invention(.identifier) is an entity type.
Printed Publication(.identifier) is an entity type.
Public Use(.identifier) is an entity type.
On Sale Activity(.identifier) is an entity type.
Effective Filing Date is a value type.

Invention was patented before its effective filing date.
Invention was described in Printed Publication before its effective filing date.
Invention was in Public Use before its effective filing date.
Invention was On Sale Activity before its effective filing date.
Invention was otherwise available to public before its effective filing date.
Invention is novel. *

* Invention is novel iff it is not true that Invention was patented before its effective filing date and it is not true that Invention was described in Printed Publication before its effective filing date and it is not true that Invention was in Public Use before its effective filing date and it is not true that Invention was On Sale Activity before its effective filing date and it is not true that Invention was otherwise available to public before its effective filing date.
