# Two possessives in one reading are not a quoted literal

Defendant(.name) is an entity type.
Plaintiff(.name) is an entity type.
Conduct(.description) is an entity type.
Land(.parcel) is an entity type.

Defendant has Conduct.
Plaintiff has possessory interest in Land.
Defendant's Conduct causes substantial and unreasonable interference with Plaintiff's use and enjoyment of Land.
Defendant is liable for Private Nuisance. *

* Defendant is liable for Private Nuisance iff Defendant's Conduct causes substantial and unreasonable interference with Plaintiff's use and enjoyment of Land and Plaintiff has possessory interest in Land.
