### label on one line: `Information Notice has Language Quality. Fact Type. *
### Verbalization: Each Information Notice has some Language Quality
### characterising its form.` The note was read as a second fact type over the
### same players (VerbalizationEachInformationNoticeHasSome...), and the
### deontic note below it as a third (2026-09-09). No FORML sentence opens
### with a word and a colon; the note is refused and reported, the reading
### before it stands alone, and the fragment "Fact Type." is unrecognized.
